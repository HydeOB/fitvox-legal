Delete your Fitvox data

Fitvox: AI Fit Coaching, published by OB App. Last updated: 18 November 2026

Fitvox has no user accounts with an email or a password. Your data is stored on your phone, and our server only knows a random account id. You can delete everything yourself, in the app:

  1. Open Fitvox and go to Profile.
  2. Tap My data.
  3. Tap Delete and confirm.

What is deleted

  • On our server: your random account id, your daily usage counts and your subscription status. This happens at once.
  • On your phone: your profile, check-ins, workouts, meals and meal photos, weight and measurements, cycle data, coach chat, reminders and settings.

What is kept

  • Total usage counts per hour, which carry no account id and cannot be linked to you.
  • A paid subscription is not cancelled by deleting your data: cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

If you no longer have the app

Uninstalling Fitvox removes all the data on your phone. To also delete the random account id on our server, write to omar@data-analyst.be from the address of your choice. Because we hold no email or name, tell us roughly when you last used the app; we answer within one month.

Fitvox is published by OB App, Belgium. Contact: omar@data-analyst.be
