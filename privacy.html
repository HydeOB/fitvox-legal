Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Fitvox keeps your data on your phone. We have no user accounts with an email or a password, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell your data. Some features use an AI coach: for those, only what the coach needs for that answer goes to our server, and our server does not keep it.

Who we are

Fitvox is published by OB App, Belgium ("we"). We are the data controller for the data described here. Contact: omar@data-analyst.be.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored on your phone only:

  • your first name, goal, level, equipment, the limits you declare (for example knee pain) and the health situations the coach notes during setup (for example pregnancy or a recent injury);
  • your daily check-ins (energy, sleep, stress, soreness), your workouts, sets and weights lifted;
  • your body weight and measurements, if you choose to track them;
  • your meals and meal photos;
  • your cycle, if you turn cycle tracking on;
  • your steps, read from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone) if you allow it. Fitvox only reads your steps. It never writes to these apps and never sends your steps anywhere;
  • your chat with the coach and your settings.

Our server does not store your messages, photos or the coach's answers. It keeps only:

  • your random account id;
  • how many times each AI feature was used that day, deleted after the day ends;
  • whether your subscription is active;
  • total usage counts per hour with no account id, to follow our costs.

Anthropic processes your request only to write the answer. Under its commercial terms it does not use this data to train its models and keeps it only for a limited time.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. We do not use analytics, advertising or crash-tracking services.

Why we may use this data (GDPR)

  • To provide the app you asked for (contract), including the AI coach and your subscription.
  • Health data (health situations, check-ins, meals, steps) is used only with your explicit consent, which you give when you enter it or allow access. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting the data or turning the feature off.

Transfers outside the EU

Anthropic and RevenueCat are in the USA. These transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete it, use "Delete my data", or uninstall the app.
  • On our server: daily usage counts until the end of the day; your account id and subscription status until you delete your data.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to its use, and withdraw your consent.

  • Export: Profile, then My data, then Export.
  • Delete: Profile, then My data, then Delete. This deletes your account on our server and everything on your phone. A paid subscription is not cancelled by this: cancel it in Google Play or the App Store. See how to delete your data.
  • For any other request, write to omar@data-analyst.be. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Belgium it is the Autorité de protection des données (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Age

Fitvox is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly process data of anyone under 18.

Security

All data sent by the app travels encrypted (HTTPS). The AI keys stay on our server, and the records on our server cannot be read by other users.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
